三个视角：PAD、G、XPThree lenses: PAD, G and XP

这里写的是引擎实际使用的方法：量程、权重、锚点、补值和校准规则。表格直接从内置 default 预设生成，与代码一致。This is the method the engine actually uses: scales, weights, anchors, gap filling and calibration. The tables are generated from the bundled default preset, so they match the code.

PAD、G、XP 都是单人纵向的反思辅助，不是临床量表，也不是对一个人的客观真值。缺失永远不算 0；AI 的推断永远标明是推断。PAD, G and XP are single-person, longitudinal reflection aids, not clinical scales and not an objective truth about a person. Missing is never 0, and an AI inference is always labelled as one.

PAD情绪的形状The shape of mood

愉悦 P、激活 A、掌控 D 三个轴，每个轴是 0–5 的连续值。Pleasure P, Arousal A and Dominance D, each a continuous value from 0 to 5.

  • 读数可以带精确时间（HH:MM）或只标时段（早、中、下午、其他）。只标时段的读数画在固定位置，图注会说明。A reading has an exact time (HH:MM) or only a slot (morning, noon, afternoon, other). Slot-only readings are drawn at fixed positions and the caption says so.
  • 曲线是高斯核局部线性拟合：一日图带宽 2.5 小时，一周图带宽 0.75 天，结果裁剪到 0–5。没有散点，没有虚线。Lines are Gaussian-kernel local linear fits: 2.5-hour bandwidth for a day, 0.75 days for a week, clamped to 0-5. No dots, no dashed lines.
  • 27 个情绪词只是粗投影：每个轴四舍五入后 0–2 为低、3 为中、4–5 为高。词永远不替代连续值。The 27 mood words are only a coarse projection: after rounding, 0-2 is low, 3 is mid and 4-5 is high on each axis. A word never replaces the continuous values.

缺失与补值Missing values and gap filling

  • 一组四项都有才算该组，两组都有才算 G；否则显示为「缺」，不当作 0。计算中不取整，只在显示时保留一位小数。A group is computed only when all four items exist, and G only when both groups exist; otherwise it shows n/a, never 0. Nothing is rounded during computation; display uses one decimal.
  • 补值按固定顺序逐项进行：historical_bridge（前后真实值线性插值）→ continuity_carry_forward（沿用最近的前值）→ scale_prior（5.0）。补值一律低置信度，带上方法和邻近日期，整天不进入训练和准确率统计。Gaps are filled per item in a fixed order: historical_bridge (linear interpolation between real values) → continuity_carry_forward (nearest earlier value) → scale_prior (5.0). Fills are always low confidence, carry their method and neighbour dates, and keep the whole day out of training and accuracy.
  • 后来的真实值改变了补值时，追加一个新修订，旧修订保留。When a later real value changes a fill, a new revision is appended and old revisions stay.

先冻结，后校准Freeze first, calibrate later

  • 每天的估计先冻结成快照。只有在快照之后表达的反馈才能关联到它；只有用户明确给出的数字才是标签，文字只作证据。Each day's estimate is frozen into a snapshot first. Only feedback expressed after the snapshot links to it; only numbers the user explicitly gave are labels, text is evidence only.
  • 反馈可以针对细项、B、E 或 G。设计向量：细项是单位向量，B/E 是该组权重，G 是两组权重各乘 0.5。Feedback may target an item, B, E or G. Design vectors: a unit vector for an item, the group weights for B or E, and both groups' weights x 0.5 for G.
  • Ridge 回归为每个细项拟合一个加性偏移：λ = 5；样本权重 2−Δ天/60（半衰期 60 天）；同一天的标注合计权重为 1；偏移裁剪到 ±2，校准后分数裁剪到 0–10。Ridge regression fits one additive offset per item: λ = 5; sample weight 2−Δdays/60 (60-day half-life); the labels of one day share a total weight of 1; offsets are clipped to ±2 and corrected scores to 0-10.
  • 前瞻评估：前 10 个标注日之后的每一天，只用更早的日子拟合再预测。MAE 先在一天内平均，再跨天平均。快照晚于当天次日冻结的回顾性回填可以参与训练，但不计入前瞻准确率。Prospective evaluation: each labelled day after the first 10 is predicted with offsets fitted only on earlier days. MAE is averaged within a day first, then across days. Retrospective backfills (snapshots frozen later than the next day) may train but never count towards prospective accuracy.
  • 门槛：至少 10 个训练日 + 10 个前瞻测试日，且 MAE 改善 ≥ 0.2 才启用；最近 10 个测试日恶化 ≥ 0.3 就停用。Gate: at least 10 training days + 10 prospective test days and an MAE gain of ≥ 0.2 to switch on; a loss of ≥ 0.3 over the last 10 test days switches it off.
  • 不同评分标准版本的标注不混用。版本变化必须升级版本号，不静默重算历史。时区默认 America/Chicago，可在 trace.toml 中修改。Labels from different rubric versions are never mixed. Any change bumps the version; history is never silently recomputed. The default timezone is America/Chicago and can be changed in trace.toml.

三者共同的规则Rules shared by all three

  • 每个数值都标明来源：self_report（你说的）、model_inference（AI 推断）或 gap_fill（补值），并带置信度。Every value states its provenance: self_report, model_inference or gap_fill, with a confidence.
  • 你的自评和 AI 的估计不一致时，两个数都保留，AI 写明理由，不悄悄改成一致。When your rating and the AI's estimate differ, both are kept and the AI states why; neither is quietly changed to match.
  • 原话逐字保存，AI 只写自己的区块。Your words are kept verbatim; the AI only writes its own blocks.
