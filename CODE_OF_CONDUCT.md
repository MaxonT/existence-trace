# Code of Conduct

Be kind and be specific. This project deals with people's private writing, so respect and care come first.

- Treat other people with respect. No harassment, personal attacks or discrimination.
- Disagree with the idea, not the person. Say what you tried and what you saw.
- Assume good faith and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Do not post other people's private information, and do not paste your own private journal text into public issues.

If someone breaks these rules, a maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or block the account. To report a problem, open an issue that says a conduct report is needed, without including private details, and a maintainer will reply with the next step.

existence-trace is not a clinical tool. If you are struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or a professional.
